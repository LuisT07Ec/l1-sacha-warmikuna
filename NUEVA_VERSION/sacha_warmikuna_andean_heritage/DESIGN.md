## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the warmth, human connection, and ancestral grounding of community-based cultural tourism in Pijal, Otavalo, Ecuador. The visual mood reconciles organic tactile authenticity with modern high-conversion mobile design. 

The aesthetic is an editorial fusion of **Warm Earth Minimalism** and **Tactile Storytelling**. It balances deep indigenous forest greens and baked terracotta clay against soft, unbleached cotton and warm cream surfaces. The experience avoids generic cold SaaS patterns or sterile corporate layouts, leaning into generous whitespace, rounded organic contours, rich typography, and reassuring, high-trust transactional elements (badges, secure form containers, and responsive WhatsApp action points).

## Colors
The palette is rooted in the landscape of Otavalo and the artisanal textiles of the Cayambi culture.

- **Primary (`#D46A37` / Terracotta):** Represents the Andean earth, hand-fired clay pottery, and vibrant woven textiles. Used exclusively for primary call-to-actions, vital interaction states, and focal moments.
- **Secondary (`#1C3B2B` / Deep Forest Green):** Evokes the Andean mountain foliage, medicinal gardens, and ancestral wisdom. Used for grounding hero containers, deep contrast sections, key headlines, and primary structural accents.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` / Direct Connection Green):** Dedicated specifically to WhatsApp integration, community messaging, and real-time support, ensuring recognizable, immediate trust.
- **Neutral (`#1F2421` / Charcoal Slate):** Replaces harsh pure black to deliver soft, high-contrast, fatigue-free reading across all editorial body copy.
- **Surfaces:** Layered using unbleached cream (`#FAF7F2`, `#F4EFE6`) for page canvas backgrounds, with crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) reserved for floating cards, elevation tiers, and lead-generation panels to maximize legibility.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy balances cultural authority with clean digital clarity.

- **Editorial Headings (`Merriweather`):** Applied to top-level section titles and emotional storytelling headlines. The organic serif character evokes literature, tradition, and respect for oral ancestral histories.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied across all body paragraphs, inputs, navigation, badges, and microcopy. Its warm, open counters provide high legibility in mobile formats.
- **Section Kicker Sub-headings (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.08em`) using terracotta or forest green tones to organize long storytelling flows into digestible chapters.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a mobile-first, single-column fluid storytelling spine that broadens into a structured multi-column container on larger viewports.

- **Mobile Viewports (< 768px):** Single-column layout with 16px lateral canvas margins (`margin`). Cards and interactive sections span the full width or maintain tight 16px interior padding to maximize touch targets and viewable content area.
- **Tablet / Desktop Viewports (≥ 768px):** Centered max-width container (constrained at 1120px for marketing content, 560px for dedicated single-column booking/forms). Spacing expands to 32px or 48px to allow imagery and cultural copy to breathe.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px/8px modular base. Related micro-elements (icons, badges, stars) use `space-xs` (8px), card interior elements use `space-md` to `space-lg` (16px–24px), and thematic sections are demarcated with `space-2xl` (48px) gaps.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this design system rejects clinical drop shadows in favor of ambient, warm-tinted organic depth and physical surface contrast.

- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas sits at ground zero with warm cream (`#FAF7F2`). Structural modules and interactive cards sit one tier above on crisp white (`#FFFFFF`), delimited by a soft 1px border (`#E5DDD0`) to convey hand-crafted, tangible quality without visual noise.
- **Ambient Earth Shadows:** When elevation is required for interactive cards and floating action elements, shadows are diffused and tinted with an earthy neutral tone (`rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06)` with a 16px to 24px blur and 4px vertical offset), avoiding harsh grays.
- **Floating Modals & CTAs:** Sticky WhatsApp anchors and booking triggers sit at high z-index with a concentrated ambient spread (`0px 8px 24px rgba(28, 59, 43, 0.18)`), grounding them as urgent, tactile actions over page content.

## Shapes
The system utilizes a balanced roundedness (`roundedness: 2`) that softens strict geometry to feel welcoming and approachable.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Styled with `rounded-lg` (16px) to `rounded-xl` (24px) corners, echoing the soft edges of hand-woven tapestries and rustic adobe architecture.
- **Pill Badges & Buttons:** Primary interactive triggers, tag labels (e.g., "Vivencial", "Cupos Limitados"), and floating action components feature complete pill rounding (9999px) for tactile, finger-friendly affordance.
- **Form Inputs:** Feature 12px to 14px rounded corners to balance structural stability with a gentle touch.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in terracotta (`#D46A37`) with pure white bold text, pill rounding, minimum height of 52px for accessibility, and subtle inner warmth. Hover state shifts to `#B34A1B` with smooth spring transition.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Styled in `#25D366` with white text and the official messaging icon. Floats fixed at the lower right corner on mobile devices with an organic breathing animation.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons:** Outlined in deep forest green (`#1C3B2B`) or soft earthy borders with medium-weight typography for secondary navigational tasks.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Category Indicators (Vivencial, Cultural, Naturaleza):** Rendered with terracotta or sage backgrounds at 10%–15% opacity, paired with high-contrast saturated text. Positioned overlaid on top-left image corners with 12px safe margins.
- **Trust Badges:** Compact pill chips featuring warm amber tones (`#FDEED9`), dark amber text (`#B34A1B`), and micro fire/star icons indicating scarcity and intimate group sizes.

### Cards & Storytelling Blocks
- **Experience Cards:** Stacked vertical media layout on mobile. High-resolution imagery with rounded top corners, followed by clean white content body containing an iconography lead-in, bold Merriweather sub-heading, and concise cultural summary.
- **Testimonial Reviews:** Crisp white surface cards with subtle `#E5DDD0` outer stroke, 5-star golden yellow rating glyphs, italicized quote body, and avatar cluster showing verified traveler details.

### Form Inputs & High-Trust Conversion
- **Lead Capture Fields:** Tall 50px input fields with light cream backgrounds (`#FAF7F2`), subtle inset borders, and neutral placeholder copy. Active focus state triggers a 2px terracotta (`#D46A37`) ring with 0px ambient bleed.
- **Consent & Guarantee Checkboxes:** Custom rounded 20px inputs paired with explicit privacy microcopy ensuring compliance and security under local tourism guidelines.
